stars.bin
  The 9096 stars of the Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised Edition (D. Hoffleit,
  W. H. Warren Jr., 1991, Yale University Observatory; catalogue V/50 of the
  astronomical data centers): place for the equator and equinox of J2000,
  visual magnitude and color index B-V. File bsc5.dat from
  http://tdc-www.harvard.edu/catalogs/bsc5.html, converted with
  tools\stars\convert.py. The 14 entries without a place (novae and objects
  that are no stars) are left out. The source states no terms of use; the
  catalogue is distributed freely by the astronomical data centers.

  A larger catalogue is not included: a file stars.bin next to Sunbeam.exe
  takes the place of this one. convert.py writes it from an extract of the
  Hipparcos catalogue (ESA, 1997, The Hipparcos and Tycho Catalogues, ESA
  SP-1200), which ESA distributes under CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO with the credit
  "ESA": https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/hipparcos/catalogues

milkyway.jpg
  The glow of the Milky Way: milkyway_2020_4k.exr from "Deep Star Maps 2020",
  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851, made from the star catalogues Hipparcos-2,
  Tycho-2 and Gaia DR2. Converted with tools\stars\convert.py: right ascension
  to the right from 0 h, brightest region at 1, stored as the square root of
  the brightness.
  Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
  Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC. Public domain (work of NASA).
