Models

What moves through the image: two ships, a sailing dinghy, an airliner and a
gull. Each is a model made by someone else, converted with
tools\models\convert.py into a mesh (.mesh) and its surface colours (.png). The
conversion reduces the number of triangles and the size of the textures, keeps
only the base colours, scales the model to metres, puts ships on their water
line and adds the positions of their lamps. Other changes are noted per model.
